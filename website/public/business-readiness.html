Customer and supplier agreements

Use entity-specific agreements reviewed by qualified counsel: scope, acceptance criteria, payment and tax terms, delivery, cancellations, refunds, warranty, data handling, proportionate liability allocation, insurance and dispute resolution. Do not promise total immunity. Verify legal entity name, address, registrations and designated complaint contacts before publishing them.

Ten role workflow

Assign a human accountable owner to each planning role. Sales drafts an inquiry → operations checks capacity → procurement obtains quotes → finance verifies tax and credit → owner approves commitment → fulfillment confirms dispatch → customer support follows up. Legal/compliance reviews exceptions; IT maintains access and audit logs; growth reviews approved metrics. AI personas draft and summarize; they do not hire staff, sign contracts, make payments or file taxes.

Privacy

The operations planner stores tasks in this browser's localStorage. It is not an authenticated or shared employee workspace. Avoid personal or confidential information. Use each task's Remove button to erase it. Static hosting and external image providers may process connection data under their own policies. Links to retailers take you to separate services. No card or banking credentials should be entered here.

Required before real operations

Authenticated staff accounts with roles, durable task records, audit logs, verified company registrations, authenticated ERP and AI endpoints, approved payment controls, backup and recovery, customer service ownership, privacy notices and appropriate insurance. None of these are certified as active by this preview.

Reference: Consumer Protection Act, 2019. Counsel and a chartered accountant should assess the actual business model and applicable central, state and local rules.
