RDNT Sparkle Industries LLP acts purely as an intermediary technological conduit under Section 79 of the Information Technology Act, 2000 for SparkDeal Hub. We do not initiate data transmission, select receivers, or tamper with third-party merchant transmissions. All product warranties and deliveries remain the exclusive legal responsibility of the selling merchants.

⚖️ 02. Consumer Protection & E-Commerce Rules

Under the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020, SparkDeal Hub operates as an inventory-free aggregator and price comparison directory. We do not handle consumer payments, collect card credentials, issue invoices, or arbitrate merchant return disputes. Consumers contract directly with the destination merchant.

🤝 03. Trademark Protection & Fair Use

Amazon, Flipkart, and other trademark assets referenced are recognized under Section 30 of the Trade Marks Act, 1999 (nominative fair use). Commercial entities agree that RDNT Sparkle Industries LLP is immune from claims relating to price adjustments, coupon expirations, or merchant conduct.

This website does not store credit card numbers, CVVs, UPI PINs, or banking credentials. When a user clicks "Get Deal", they are redirected to the merchant's secure, 256-bit TLS encrypted checkout portal. Standard server logging complies with Indian Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act benchmarks.
