Helpful starting points.

How can I make meals simpler?

Choose a few repeatable breakfasts and build lunches around vegetables, a protein food and a grain or other staple you enjoy. The free planner gives practical combinations; check your own allergy and dietary needs.

Build a starter week ↗
How can I get more consistent with movement?

Choose activities you enjoy and build gradually. Some activity is better than none. At the gym, ask a qualified trainer to help with equipment and technique rather than guessing.

Read CDC activity guidance ↗
How does the fashion section help?

Use outfit formulas, colour combinations and pieces you already own to make everyday dressing easier. Lifestyle Hub includes a style library; it does not require customers to buy a new wardrobe.

Will paid plans unlock when I click Enroll?

No. Access must be granted only after Stripe payment and the subscription are verified by a secure server. Enrollment is not active on the current deployment, and this app does not collect card details.
